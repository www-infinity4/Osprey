## Key Concepts

### The Velocity Intercept
The Osprey sees Differential Speeds, not matter.  It measures the lag of a
`ThermalStream` from the Source Singularity, matches its velocity, and applies
Octave Consonance to convert violent de-synchronized resets into Harmonic Flow.

### Mirror-Heading Tracker
Charge is treated as Directional Velocity.  A **Proton** is an Outbound pulse;
an **Electron** is an Inbound hunter.  When `sync_mirror_streams` detects that
their superposition produces a **Purple** standing wave, the Hydrogen Door is
forced open (`NODE_LOCKED`) and a `HeliumNode` is minted as coolant.

### 8-Note Quantum Sign-In
Users authenticate by playing an 8-note sequence.  The system stores the
**BPM offset** and **Velocity (Dynamics)** of each strike as a Signature
Frequency.  A **70 % dynamic match** grants access — organic human touch that
a deterministic replay cannot reproduce.
